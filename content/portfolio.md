## 1. Food Insecurity & Maternal Mortality in Mali

**Field:** Development Economics
**Tools:** STATA, statistical analysis, research

### Overview

This research project examined the relationship
between food insecurity and maternal mortality
in Mali. It explored how economic and social
conditions can affect maternal health outcomes.

### Methods

* Worked with economic and demographic data.
* Used STATA to analyze variables and investigate
  relationships.
* Reviewed research and interpreted statistical results.

### Skills Demonstrated

Data cleaning, statistical analysis, research,
economic reasoning, and written communication.
