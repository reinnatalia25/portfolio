<h2>Healthcare Outcomes Analysis:<br>
WIC Participation & Academic Performance</h2>

<p class="project-course">
Economics of Health & Healthcare
</p>

<h3>Overview</h3>

<p>
This project examined the relationship between state-level WIC participation
rates and fourth-grade standardized reading and mathematics performance.
The goal was to evaluate potential relationships between participation in
a major nutrition assistance program and longer-term educational outcomes.
</p>

<p>
I organized and analyzed healthcare and education datasets using Microsoft
Excel, identified trends in the data, and communicated the findings through
a research paper and presentation.

<p class="project-category">ECONOMIC RESEARCH · STATA</p>

<h2>Historical Tourism & Occupational Segregation Analysis</h2>

<p class="project-course">
American Economic Mobility
</p>

<h3>Overview</h3>

<p>
This research project examined the relationship between tourism development
and occupational segregation within Virginia's Historic Triangle using
historical Census microdata.
</p>

<p>
I cleaned and analyzed IPUMS Census data using STATA, performed descriptive
and regression analysis, and created statistical tables and visualizations
to examine demographic and employment patterns.
</p>

<h3>Tools & Methods</h3>

<span>IPUMS</span>
